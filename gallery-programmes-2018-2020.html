How the calculation works

Assign each artist to their first curated solo or named group exhibition in that gallery’s programme. Use the opening year, including for shows that continue into January. Count a guest presentation only for a gallery with documented programming credit. Check earlier exhibitions, off-site activity and the artist’s CV. The first professional public show must fall in the intake year or the preceding five calendar years. An earlier qualifying museum outcome or benchmark signing excludes the artist.

Follow each eligible artist from the day after gallery entry through the fifth anniversary. Qualifying events are a curated exhibition or permanent acquisition by a public-serving nonprofit museum, or new representation by Gagosian, David Zwirner, Hauser & Wirth or Pace. Artist and museum records can supply different parts of the evidence. Private holdings, retail listings and fair appearances alone earn no credit.

A rate uses every eligible entrant in three complete annual censuses. A tier also requires at least ten entrants and verified outcomes or five-year reviews for at least 80%. An unresolved outcome stays in a complete census’s denominator. An unresolved entrant count leaves the three-year rate unavailable. A year before a gallery’s founding can have a verified zero.

Under framework 1.0, Measured clears those evidence gates. Promising additionally needs two successful artists and a 95% Wilson lower bound of at least 10%. Proven needs at least 15 entrants, a lower bound of at least 20%, at least three artists with museum outcomes and successes in at least two intake years. The lower bound treats unresolved follow-up as unsuccessful. The tier describes subsequent artist careers and carries no estimate of resale proceeds.
